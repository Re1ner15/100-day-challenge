## Progression philosophy (deficit-adjusted)
1. **Hold the load.** Same weight × reps week to week = muscle maintained. That's the primary win.
2. **Double progression.** Work a rep range. Add reps first; only add weight (2.5 kg small / 2.5–5 kg big lifts) once you hit the top of the range on *all* sets with 2+ reps in reserve (RIR).
3. **Autoregulate.** Bad sleep / low energy week → repeat, don't force. Repeating is not failure in a cut.
4. **Deload ~Week 8** (and again if InBody flags trouble): cut volume/load ~40% for one week.
5. **Protein is non-negotiable:** aim ~1.6–2.2 g per kg bodyweight daily to protect muscle.

## The InBody guardrail — how we read the scan
- **Fat mass ↓, lean mass steady** → 🎯 perfect. Keep going.
- **Fat mass ↓, lean mass slowly ↓** → acceptable early, but if it continues 2–3 wks: eat more protein, slightly raise calories, maybe drop one cardio session.
- **Nothing moving for 2 weeks** → tighten deficit slightly (small food cut, not more cardio).
- **Strength dropping fast + lean mass ↓** → deficit too aggressive. Eat more. Fat loss you can't hold isn't progress.
