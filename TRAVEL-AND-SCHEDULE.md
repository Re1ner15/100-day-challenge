## Travel playbook (Ratnagiri especially)
**On the road, maintenance is a win.** You do NOT need a deficit while traveling — holding steady, protein high, and not gaining fat is a total success. Trying to run a strict cut on work travel is how the plan gets abandoned.

**Diet — protein first:**
- **Carry whey sachets** — portable protein insurance; you can hit ~100–120 g even with zero good restaurant options.
- Default order: **chicken / eggs / fish / paneer + veg.** Skip the bread basket, desserts, sugary drinks, alcohol.
- Aim ~maintenance (~2,400–2,700 kcal), **protein ≥ 140 g.** Not gaining fat = winning.

**Movement:**
- **Walk** — steps are the win you can get anywhere (aim 6+ km/day).
- No gym? **Hotel-room circuit:** push-ups, bodyweight squats, walking lunges, plank — 2–3× keeps the habit and the muscle-retention signal alive.
- Or find a local gym for a quick 20–30 min session.

**Mindset:** a *maintained* week on the road beats a "perfect" plan you quit. Log it honestly, come back, resume. No compensation binges/crashes after — just pick the plan back up.
