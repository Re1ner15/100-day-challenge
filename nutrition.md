# Nutrition

Fat loss is driven by the **calorie deficit**; muscle is protected by **protein**. Your [scan history](./logs/inbody-history.md) proves it — every time protein stayed high, SMM held at ~38 kg through the cut. That's the whole game.

## 🎯 Daily targets (for the 85 kg stretch goal)
| Target | Amount | Notes |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Protein** | **160–180 g** | non-negotiable — the muscle guardian |
| **Calories** | **~1,700–1,900 kcal** | steep but sustainable (NOT ~1,000 — that loses muscle & rebounds) |
| Carbs | moderate, **around the workout** | rice / oats / banana / potato → fuels the lifts |
| Fat | fill the remainder (~40–55 g) | watch hidden fat (full-fat paneer, oil) |
| Fibre | isabgol + veggies daily | satiety + digestion |
| Creatine | **5 g daily** | retains strength/muscle in a deficit — take every day |
| Water | 3–4 L | |

> **Efficiency rule:** in a steep cut, get protein from **low-calorie sources** (whey, egg whites, chicken, low-fat paneer, Greek yogurt). Full-fat paneer costs ~16 kcal per g of protein vs ~5 for whey — use it sparingly.
