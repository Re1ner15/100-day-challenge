**Key metrics to watch (in priority order):**
1. **Skeletal Muscle Mass (SMM)** — want this flat or up. This is the muscle-maintenance scoreboard.
2. **Body Fat Mass** — want this steadily down.
3. **Body Fat %** — the summary of the two above.

Don't panic over single-week swings (water/sodium/carbs move these). Read the **3-week trend**.
